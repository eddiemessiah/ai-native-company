Agents can write a thousand outreach messages before lunch. The hard part is making sure none of them goes out wrong, or without you.

The GTM Harness is my answer, and it's free and open source:

1. Any model writes the plan and the first drafts.
2. A reviewer marks each draft ready, revise or blocked. It can stop a draft; it can't send one.
3. Code checks the drafts against your own rules: invented numbers, unfilled [slots], banned phrases, opt-outs. Each finding comes with its fix.
4. You approve in Telegram. Change one word and it needs your yes again.
5. One tap opens WhatsApp, email or X with the message filled in. You send it.

Every correction you make becomes a rule, so the drafts get closer to how you write.
