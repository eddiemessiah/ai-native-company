# Thread 1: Launch (pin this)

1/
I'm building an AI-native firm in Lagos.

Not an agency. Not a SaaS.

A firm that sells finished work (audits, agents, grant applications, company brains), where agents do the work and people own the outcome.

It's called Nova. Here's how it works 🧵

2/
The $120k accountant vs the $10k QuickBooks problem:

For 20 years software fought over the tool budget. The money was always in the *service* budget, locked behind people.

AI unlocks it. You can sell the closed books, not the bookkeeping software. At software margins.

3/
So every job we sell has a unit, never an hour:

• one audit
• one agent in production
• one grant application
• one company brain

Priced against what the human alternative costs, not our costs.

4/
Inside every job, we split the work three ways:

🟡 LLM writes: drafts, briefs, code
🔵 System One decides: route, score, approve (typed answers + calibrated confidence)
⚪ Code executes: counts, dates, payments, records
🔴 A person approves anything with money, legal or reputation attached

5/
Why the split matters:

Most calls inside an agent aren't text. They're "which team?", "is this urgent?", "does this need approval?"

We stopped renting a frontier model to say "billing".

A System One model answers all of those in one call, for a fraction of the cost.

6/
Every decision gets logged: model version, probabilities, confidence, who approved.

Regulators in Ghana and Nigeria are moving from "use AI responsibly" to "show me the evidence".

We ship the evidence with every job.

7/
Agents are customers too.

Our decision recipes are HTTP endpoints agents pay for per call in USDC on Celo, with x402. No account. No API key. The request is the transaction.

8/
And we train the people who run the agents.

The AI Study Group becomes a real group: pods, live cohorts, city chapters (Lagos → Accra → Nairobi → the diaspora), certificates on Celo, and paid work on our jobs for the best builders.

9/
Shipped before we had a name:

• Omni402: x402 settled on Celo mainnet
• ERC-8004 agent #9765
• 22 projects into Proof of Ship in 2 months
• 25+ events, 11+ workshops
