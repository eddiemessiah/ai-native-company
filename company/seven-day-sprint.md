# The 7-day revenue sprint (Sat 26 Sep → Sat 3 Oct 2026)

**Goal: real money in the account by day 7, from people who already know you, while setting up the grant and credit applications that land later.**

The fastest money isn't a new product. It's selling finished work to your existing network: website clients, builders, founders and event contacts. Everything below uses assets that exist today: the site, the brain, Omni402, the academy and your track record.

## Rules for the sprint

1. **Deposit before work.** 50% upfront, except the five free design-partner audits.
2. **A fixed scope in writing, one unit, one price.** No hourly work, no "let's see how it goes".
3. **The brain routes every lead** (the site intake), and a person replies within a working day.
4. **Log every mistake.** It's the most valuable output of week one.
5. **Stay inside the conflict rules.** No paid work for builders you support in your Celo role; paid grant work only for programs you have no part in.
