# Conflicts of interest

The founder is also Celo's Regional Ambassador for Africa (working with the Celo Core Co. DevRel team), Partnership Manager at Nigeria Blockchain Week, and Program Manager at Ethereum Nigeria. Those roles are part of why the firm has distribution, and exactly why it needs clear rules. **Trust is the moat; don't spend it.**

## Rules

1. **Separate identities.** The firm uses its own domain email, bank account, payment links and wallet. Never use the `@celo.org` email or ecosystem channels to sell the firm's services.
2. **No paid work for builders you support in an ecosystem role.** Builders in Proof of Ship, hackathons you judge, or programs you run get free guidance and open-source tools. The firm can sell to businesses and teams outside those programs.
3. **No paid grant work for programs you influence.** If you (or your team) review, nominate, judge or allocate for a program, the firm doesn't prepare applications to it.
4. **No self-nomination.** Programs where your own team nominates grantees (for example Prezenti Boost) are off-limits for the firm's products. The only exception is full written disclosure, your recusal, and a decision by people with no reporting line to you.
5. **Disclose, always.** Every application the firm submits to an ecosystem you're affiliated with carries a disclosure line: "The founder is {role} at {org}; he had no role in reviewing or deciding this application."
6. **Judge fairly.** When judging hackathons, declare any team the firm has worked with or pitched, and recuse from scoring it.
7. **Check your agreements.** Read your ambassador and employment agreements for exclusivity, outside-work and IP clauses before signing the first client. If unclear, ask in writing.
8. **No volume games.** Never generate on-chain activity between wallets you control to hit program metrics or impress judges.

## Disclosure template

> Disclosure: {Founder} serves as {role} at {organization}. {Organization} had no role in commissioning this work, and {Founder} took no part in reviewing, scoring or deciding {program/application}.
