# Naming the company

**Name: Nova.** Edidiong chose it on 26 Sep 2026. It lives in one file (`packages/catalog/src/brand.ts`); the site, llms.txt, the agent card and the OG image all read it from there.

> Nothing below has been checked for domains or trademarks yet. The sandbox this was written in couldn't reach registrars or trademark databases. Run the checks in the last section before buying a domain or printing anything.

## Why Nova

- **Latin for "new".** A new kind of firm: finished work sold per unit, done by agents, owned by people.
- **A nova is a star that suddenly burns thousands of times brighter.** That's the study-group story: builders who flare up once they ship.
- **Travels.** Four letters, one pronunciation from Lagos to Nairobi to London, spellable after hearing it once.
- **The agent co-founder becomes Nova One,** still a nod to System One.

## Risks to check first

Nova fails the "be ownable" test below, so the checks matter more than they would for a coined word.

- **Amazon Nova** is Amazon's family of foundation models, and **Nova Act** is its SDK for building agents. That's our category, so expect trademark friction in class 42 (software and AI services) and a crowded search page.
- Other companies use the name too, including **Nova Credit** (a US fintech). `nova.ai` and `nova.com` are almost certainly taken.
- **Mitigation:** register a distinctive mark rather than the bare word (the woven logo plus a descriptor, for example "Nova Works"). Pick one compound domain and use the same handle everywhere. Candidates to check: `novaworks.africa`, `nova.africa`, `withnova.ai`, `novahq.co`, `joinnova.ai`.

## What a name has to do

1. **Say "the work gets done".** We sell finished work, not tools or hours.
2. **Carry two audiences.** Businesses buying work, and learners becoming the people who run agents.
3. **Travel.** Lagos first, then Accra, Nairobi and the diaspora: pronounceable everywhere, spellable after hearing it once.
4. **Be ownable.** Not a common English word, and no collision in African fintech or AI.

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Root | Why it works | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fundi** (the previous working name) | Kiswahili *fundi*, the craftsperson who does the job for you; isiZulu *umfundi*, a learner | Covers both halves: the firm that ships work and the school that trains the people who ship it | FundiBots (Ugandan robotics education) and several "Fundi" marketplace apps exist |
| **Ukeme** | Ibibio: "ability, capability" | Your own heritage; unique; "capability, on demand" | Opaque outside Akwa Ibom; spelling on first hearing |
| **Oya** | Nigerian: "let's go, get moving" | Instant energy; three letters; "Say oya. It's done." | Also an Orisha name; many "Oya" businesses exist |
| **Tenda** | Kiswahili: "to act, to do" | Agents that act; short and global | Less known; check collisions |
| **Kazi** | Kiswahili: "work, job" | Literally "the work" | Common word; crowded namespace |
| **Nsibidi** | The ideographic script of the Cross River and Akwa Ibom region | A language of symbols, and our product is typed decisions | Hard to spell; better as a product name (see below) |
| **Anansi** | Akan storyteller spider, weaver of webs | A web of agents; Accra resonance | Trickster connotation is wrong for a firm that owns its mistakes |
| **Àṣẹ (Ashe)** | Yoruba: "the power to make things happen" | Strong meaning for an agentic firm | Sacred in Ifá and Orisha traditions; some clients may object |
| **Unitwork** | English, from "sell the unit, never the hour" | The business model in the name | Generic; weak cultural signal |
| **Night Shift** | English | Agents work while you sleep | Likely taken; tone may read as gimmicky |
| **Declan** | Your existing Declan Work brand | Continuity with a brand you already own | Tied to Web3 talent; no meaning to carry |

## Checks before you commit

1. Domains: the candidates above, plus `.com`, `.ai`, `.work`, `.africa`, `.ng` and `.com.ng` variants.
2. Nigerian trademark search (Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry) in classes 35, 41 and 42; add Ghana and Kenya when you expand. Search for "Nova" marks in class 42 first.
3. CAC business-name availability.
4. Handles on X, Instagram, LinkedIn, GitHub, Telegram and WhatsApp Business.
5. Say it out loud to ten people in Lagos, Accra and London; ask them to spell it back.
