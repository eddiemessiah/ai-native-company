# Outbound scripts

Short, specific, and each ends in a single ask. Send on the channel the person already uses with you (WhatsApp for SMEs, X or Telegram for builders, email or LinkedIn for fintechs and NGOs). Personalize the first line every time; never paste the whole script as-is.

The lead magnet is always **do the first job free, and show them their own data** (Greg calls it "sell the screenshot").

---

## 1. Existing website clients: "what AI says about you"

> Hi {name}, quick one. I asked ChatGPT and Gemini "{a buyer question, e.g. where to buy fresh eggs in Lekki}", and here's what they said about {business} 👇 [screenshot]
>
> {One line on what's wrong: "They list your old price", "You're not mentioned; two competitors are", "It says you don't deliver"}.
>
> More customers are asking AI before they call. I can fix what it says, and add a WhatsApp assistant that answers price and delivery questions and takes orders on your site while you sleep. Setup is ₦300k, then ₦35k a month. We start this week if you're in.
>
> Want the full audit (25 questions, 4 assistants) first? It's free for you as an existing client.

**Follow-up (48 hours later):** "Two more of your competitors showed up when I re-asked today. Want me to start Thursday?"

## 2. SMEs you don't know yet: DM on Instagram or WhatsApp

> Hi {name}, I run an AI firm in Lagos. I checked what ChatGPT tells people about {business}: it {specific error}. I'll send you the full report free, no strings. Can I send it here?

(Send the report. Then pitch the Agent-Ready Website or the monthly tracking.)

## 3. Funded startups (founders and ops leads)

>
> Two things I'm doing for teams your size right now:
> 1. A **Company Brain** in Slack that remembers decisions and answers "who owns X / what did we decide about Y", and knows when to stay quiet.

>
> If your LLM bill is growing, I also retrofit agents so routing and approvals run on a decision model at a fraction of the cost. We only charge a share of the measured savings.
>
> 20 minutes this week? {booking link}

## 4. Fintechs and banks (compliance, risk and ops leads)

> Hi {name}, with the CBN's automated-AML standards and AI auditors on the way, I'm offering fintechs two things:
>
> - An **Agent Reliability Audit** for any AI or automated decision system you run: consistency, robustness, calibration and data-leak checks, with reproducible traces your examiner can read.
> - **AML alert triage.** Alerts are scored and closed or escalated with evidence, and suspicious-transaction report drafts go to your analysts. An analyst approves every filing.
>
> Can I run a free pilot on 200 of last month's alerts (anonymized) and show you the agreement rate with your team's decisions?

## Proposal template (send within 24 hours of a call)

```
{Offer name}: {client}

What you get (the unit): {one sentence}
What we need from you (intake): {3–5 bullets}
How we check it (rulebook + review): {2–3 bullets}
Delivered as: {format} by {date}
Price: {amount}. 50% deposit to start: {payment link}. Balance on delivery.
Not included: {1–3 bullets}
```
