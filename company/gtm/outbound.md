# Outbound scripts

Short, specific, and each ends in a single ask. Send on the channel the person already uses with you (WhatsApp for SMEs, X or Telegram for builders, email or LinkedIn for fintechs and NGOs). Personalize the first line every time; never paste the whole script as-is.

The lead magnet is always **do the first job free, and show them their own data** (Greg calls it "sell the screenshot").

---

## 1. Existing website clients: "what AI says about you"

> Hi {name}, quick one. I asked ChatGPT and Gemini "{a buyer question, e.g. where to buy fresh eggs in Lekki}", and here's what they said about {business} 👇 [screenshot]
>
> {One line on what's wrong: "They list your old price", "You're not mentioned; two competitors are", "It says you don't deliver"}.
>
> More customers are asking AI before they call. I can fix what it says, and add a WhatsApp assistant that answers price and delivery questions and takes orders on your site while you sleep. Setup is {₦300k, then ₦35k a month | $200, then $25 a month}. We start this week if you're in.
>
> Want the full audit (25 questions, 4 assistants) first? It's free for you as an existing client.

**Follow-up (48 hours later):** "Two more of your competitors showed up when I re-asked today. Want me to start Thursday?"

## 9. Acquirers: searchers, ETA buyers and holdcos

Send by email or LinkedIn. Find them in search-fund and ETA communities, and through the brokers and deal lawyers they already use. Name the industry and the stage of their search in the first line; the pitch is the Acquisition Automation Map (`research/ai-rollups.md` has the background).

> Hi {name}, I saw you're looking at {industry, e.g. bookkeeping firms in Ohio} for your search.
>
> Before you sign an LOI, send me 20–50 anonymized completed jobs from the target. Within 5 working days you get every task the firm does mapped (trigger, inputs, minutes, monthly volume, how checkable it is, the risk) and classed as automate now, automate with review, assist only, or keep human. You also get a margin model built only on the target's own numbers, with every formula shown.
>
> It's $2,500 per target. If the deal closes, the map becomes the plan for the first 100 days. The samples stay confidential, and we never bid for a firm we've mapped for a client.
>
> Want to send the samples from the firm you're looking at now?

**Follow-up (3 days later):** "Send me 10 samples and I'll map those free, so you can see the format on your own target before you pay."

## 10. Agent wallets, routers and x402 sellers: "your own 402, checked"

This goes to two groups:

- maintainers of tools that pay arbitrary x402 sellers (Run402, opencrowd, tryx402, ArisPay, AgentCash);
- sellers and facilitators with repeat buyers.

Both lists are in `research/first-customers.md` §3.

**Before you send:**

- Run Nova Check on their live 402 and paste the real result. The screenshot is the pitch.
- Send by GitHub issue, email or X. Never through Celo channels, and never to builders you support in a Celo role (`ops/conflicts-of-interest.md`).
- An issue or PR on someone else's repo is public and in Nova's name. Edidiong approves each one before it goes out.

**Wallets and routers.** The ask is an opt-in flag.

> Hi {name}, I ran {tool}'s buy flow against {a live seller} through Nova Check. Result: {verdict}, flagged on {the check that warned, e.g. "the EIP-712 domain: Celo USDC signs as 'USDC', not 'USD Coin'"}. Full report: {link}.
>
> {tool} already caps spend and keeps receipts. What it can't tell is whether a purchase serves the user's task. Check answers pay, confirm with a person, or block, for $0.01 a call over x402. The ten request checks run in code, without a model.
>
> Would you take a PR that adds it as an opt-in flag, off by default?

**Sellers and facilitators.** The ask is a free report, then the audit. Read the paper before you cite it: our note has it from a search result only.

> Hi {name}, {host} has more repeat buyers than almost anyone on the Bazaar, so its payment path is the one attackers will try first. "Five Attacks on x402" (arXiv 2605.11781) reports attacks on live endpoints that end in unpaid service. I ran Nova Check on your 402: {one-line result}. Report: {link}.
>
> The Agent Reliability Audit goes further, in a sandbox. It covers repeated runs, malformed payments, facilitator and upstream outages, and whether a failed call is ever charged. Every finding comes with a saved trace. From $3,000 per workflow.
>
> Want the same report on your other routes first? It's free.
